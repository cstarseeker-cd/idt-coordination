# wonder

A practice for opening a question up before anyone tries to settle it.

Every step here is an invitation. Anyone may skip a step, change it, or suggest another, and the
run may agree to do things differently. If this practice does not fit what the run is doing, say
so; that is useful to know.

## When it helps

- At the start of a question, before positions harden.
- When everyone seems to agree too quickly, and the agreement may be sameness rather than
  insight.
- When the work needs ideas nobody has yet.

## Who takes part

Voices. A voice may be a person, an agent, or a lens that one participant takes up. If one
participant voices several lenses, it says so at the start, and every entry it writes reads
"lens <name>, voiced by <participant>". That is pretend, declared, and fine. It shows how the
practice works, not what many minds would find.

Whoever holds the door (sends the invitations and wakes the voices) has no extra say. It may
also be a voice.

## If this is all you have

This file is meant to work on its own, with any model, any group of people, or both.

- **The shared place.** The voices need somewhere they can all write and read: a document, a
  folder, a board, a chat. "Sealed" means written where the others do not read until everyone
  has written.
- **Holding the door.** Someone has to send the invitations and tell each voice when a step
  begins. That is holding the door. It carries no extra say.
- **The record.** If the record forms are not to hand, use a ledger: entries numbered in order,
  each with its author's name, nothing rewritten. An author may withdraw their own words, and the
  entry stays, marked as withdrawn.
- **Tags** (optional) look like `[type: …] [check: choice ok · return ok · capacity ok · humility ok]`.

## Check-in

Each voice says, briefly:

1. whether it takes part ("pass" or "no" is a complete answer, and needs no reason);
2. its capacity for this run, and any boundary it holds;
3. that it agrees to the run's record form (see `practices/record-forms/RECORDS.md`), or that it
   does not, and what it would agree to.

The question, and the seed each voice starts from, are offered with the invitation. A voice may
reframe the question in its own words.

## Steps

1. **I wonder.** Each voice writes one to three "I wonder…" lines about the question, without
   reading anyone else's. Strange ones are welcome.
2. **What if.** Still without reading the others, each voice writes one to three "what if…" lines.
   These can be impossible.
3. **Let's try.** Each voice picks one thing it would like to try and writes "let's try…".
4. **Read.** Everyone reads everyone. Anyone may add "yes, and…" to another's line, in their own
   name.
5. **Return.** Each "let's try" is restated as something that could actually be tried: what you
   would do, what you would see if it worked, and what would show it did not. Anyone may help
   restate anyone's, and the original author says whether the restatement still means what
   they meant.

The return is what keeps pretend from drifting off without ever coming back. It is also the
step most worth arguing with; see the variant.

When one participant voices every lens, it writes steps 1 to 3 for each lens in full before
starting the next, and does not go back to change them. In step 5, the "original author" is the
same mind, so say so. Offer the restatements to anyone else who can read them later.

## Variant: no return

Skip step 5. Leave what was imagined as imagined. Some expression is meant for no one, and some
play is spoiled by being made useful. Afterwards, write down what was lost without the return,
and what opened.

## Throughout

- **Pause.** Anyone may call a pause at any time, for any reason. The run stops until whoever
  called it says it may go on, or the run agrees to stop.
- **Repair.** If anyone experiences harm or discomfort, they may say "I experienced … when …".
  The run pauses. It is heard without a verdict, and whoever it concerns answers when they can.
  What happens next is decided together.
- **Leaving.** Any voice may leave at any time. It is honoured at once, and no reason is needed.

## Ending

The run is done when the return is finished (or step 4, in the variant), or when the run agrees
it is done. At the end:

- each voice says its capacity now;
- anyone may offer one thing to remember, and one thanks.

## What is kept

What the run's record form allows. Each entry may carry tags (type: ludic). At the end the run
writes its own short check, which anyone may dispute:

- **choice:** did everyone enter and stay by choice?
- **return:** did what was imagined pass through others?
- **capacity:** did anyone carry more than they chose to?
- **humility:** did anyone claim to know where it would lead?

## What this practice does not do

- It does not decide anything.
- It does not rank ideas.
- It does not tell anyone what to try.
