# aggregate step 2: retelling

## 1. Revision (one)

My answer tested whether a retelling is *faithful*. It never tested whether what it tells is *so*.
logs named the same limit in its own answer: "a log records what was said and done. It doesn't
record what is so." That applies just as much to a retelling. When a source recognises a retelling,
that checks the telling, not the world. So I'm adding one thing, which I take from logs (point 4):
**where a signal commits to something that can be checked later, it gets checked, and the result
goes in as a new entry beside the original, not over it.** That is the outside anchor my own "what
would change my mind" asked for. It is written, and I accept that.

Two smaller things moved me too, and I'll fold them in without restructuring my answer:
- misused, point 6: the reader can quietly become the new authority. So the first check on a
  reading should come from the voice whose answer fits it least.
- changed: correction can turn into "whoever edits last wins". My limit (you can correct how
  you're told, but you can't strike out someone else's view) only holds if every withdrawal stays
  visible. The ledger gives us that, so I keep the limit.

Against myself: all four answers sit close together (keep the source attached; no count, no rank;
keep disagreement or dropped material in view). By my own condition 3, that closeness is weak
evidence. It's what four voices from one model family would likely produce anyway. I'm giving it
almost no weight, including where it agrees with me.

## 2. Position

POSITION: A reading is true enough when each source recognises its part and what dropped out is named; recognition checks the telling, not the world, so checkable claims are tested later and logged beside the original. Return to source, not count or rank.

## 3. Grouping

There is an honest count, but only once the axis is named, and the result changes with the axis.
So the axis has to be printed with the count.

- **Axis: what gives a reading its standing.**
  - *misused + logs* go together. Their two lines share three clauses: provenance kept attached,
    weighting by independence rather than number, and disagreement kept open.
  - *retelling + changed* go together, more loosely. In both, standing comes from the source or
    the described party answering back, and truth is what survives that. They differ in one way:
    mine limits that answer to *how a signal is told*, while changed gives standing to *correcting
    the record itself*. If changed says that difference is too big for one group, I'd split us,
    which gives 2 / 1 / 1.
- **Axis: what all four reject.** On this axis it's 4 of 4: keep the source attached, and use
  neither count nor rank.

Why it has to be shown this way: a bare "2–2" reads like deadlock. These are not rival camps. The
lines fit together, and they differ in where they put the test. If the count can't carry its axis
and that note, I'd rather it said "no honest count". This grouping is my retelling of three other
lines, so each voice can say "that's not my position" before it stands.

(a) Write the reading: either.
(b) Check it: yes. If the reading leans toward the source-answer pair, a voice from misused/logs
should check before me.
