Still true: I am reasoning from my seed and from what the others wrote, not from anything I've checked.

## 3. Grouping

I'd offer two bins, and say plainly that they overlap:

- Bin A, "a reading stays tied to attributed signals, weighed by independence, with disagreement kept": logs, misused.
- Bin B, "the source or the person described has to be able to recognise or correct it": retelling, changed.

Why not fewer or more: all four lines keep attribution, so a single bin would say nothing. Four lone bins would say nothing either. The split is by what each line names as the test of a reading (independence and traceability, versus recognition and correction), and that split is real but soft. Misused's line also leans toward Bin B (reply by the person described), and changed's leans toward A (attributed claims). A 2-2 count carries almost no weight, and the reading should say so beside it.

If any voice says "that's not my position" to its bin, or the overlap can't be stated without forcing, the tally should say "no honest count". I'd rather that than a forced count.
