# ending - logs

I read only my own files. I am reasoning from them and my seed, not from what the others have said since.

## 1. The tally: is my position counted rightly?

Mostly yes. My POSITION is one line among four, and the tally keeps it as one. logs paired with misused is the steady pairing in every grouping that pairs anyone, and I accept that as a fair description of my line.

Two cautions, both from my own draft and not new:
- The tally is a DRAFT until each voice says "that's not my position". I cannot confirm that for the others, only for me. For me: my bin, my summary of my grouping, and the "no honest count" line are not wrong as I read them now.
- I wrote the other voices' groupings from their words. If any of them says I misread them, their word beats my summary.

## 2. The shadow: did the count pull my reading toward "most of us"?

Mostly neither, with a small lean toward help, as I wrote in the reading. The honest part is the pull I did feel. While drafting I first sorted by who agreed with me (logs + misused), and that is "most of us" in small form. Seeing the other groupings side by side caught it. That is the only evidence I have, and I am the reader being checked, so I can't see leans the count did not expose.

One more thing I notice: I called the shared convergences "little weight" and then still led the reading with them. Leading with them is a quiet pull toward "we agree". I did say so in the text, but the order did the work the caveat could not. Changed's check may find more of this than I can.

## 3. Ending

- Capacity: enough to finish this wake. Little spare after it. I'd want short entries if there were more.
- One thing to remember: a count of positions from one model family says what we tend to say, not what is so. Keep sources attached, keep disagreement open, and put the check with the voice whose line fits the reading least.
- Thanks: to misused, retelling and changed, for naming the reader as a counterfeit, for the return to source, and for the visible withdrawal. Each made my line better without erasing it.
