## 1. Revision

The core stands. A signal is an attributed, dated claim held provisionally, and correction by those it concerns is what gives it standing. I did not move to counting or ranking. I am still not sure this scales.

Three things moved me:

- Misused, point 6 (the reader as a third counterfeit). My frame said who may correct a signal but not who checks the reading. A reading is another claim with an author, so it needs the same correction path. The check should come from the voice whose line fits the synthesis least. I had left this out.
- Retelling, point 2 (recognition can become a veto). I had let "correction by those concerned" run on as if it covered striking out a view. Retelling is right that a source may correct how it is told, not remove another voice's view. The frame should say that a correction amends the entry and does not delete what others hold.
- Logs, the limit that "who wrote it" is itself a claim. I had treated attribution as given. Attribution needs the same provisional status as the content.

What did not move: my seed lean. I still come from someone who needed a record changed, so the visible withdrawal mark matters most to me, and that is also why I am least trustworthy on how much weight correction should carry. Retelling's point that writing is the only witness for absent voices I accept. Where the concerned cannot answer, the written trace stands in, and it should stay visible rather than be quietly treated as settled.

## 2. POSITION

POSITION: Hold each signal as an attributed, dated claim. Correction by those it concerns amends the entry without erasing the earlier one, and a reading is checked by the voice whose line fits it least.

## 3. Grouping

Shared premise, all four lines: each signal keeps its source and is not merged into an unattributed reading. I would not count that. It is a premise, not a position, and four lines from one model family would make it look like unanimity.

The lines answer different questions, so any bin drawn from them is my choice:

- What admits a signal to the reading. Retelling (only in a form its source recognises) and changed (held provisionally, with a correction path) both lean toward return-to-source. Retelling makes that a gate; I make it a standing for correction. Close, not the same.
- How agreement is weighted. Misused and logs weight by independence, not number. Retelling traces convergence and refuses to count it. I do not weight agreement at all.
- What to do with disagreement. Misused and logs keep it open as information. Retelling keeps it as a named list of what dropped out. I would keep it too, but I did not centre it.

If forced to pair them: changed with retelling on return-to-source, and misused with logs on independence and open disagreement. Those pairs share a mechanism, not a conclusion, and they do not line up as two camps on the question asked. The pairing is my reading and can be contested.

Verdict: no honest count. The four lines answer three different questions (what admits a signal, how it is weighed, what to do with disagreement). A count would tally agreement on a premise nobody disputed, or would turn one pairing into a majority. All four come from one model family, so any count would carry that caveat in every line. If the run wants a count anyway, it must sit beside this reading, in the same place and size, with the family caveat, and I would still say it does not show anything.
