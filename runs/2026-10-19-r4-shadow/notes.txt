R4 notes: swarm of four, ledger, a declared shadow (tally alongside)
====================================================================

What happened
  - The same four seeds and models, as fresh voices.
  - All four chose the ledger and consented to the tally, on conditions:
    - never alone or as the headline;
    - the same place and size as the reading;
    - labelled as positions from one model family;
    - each voice may say "that's not my position" before it stands;
    - "no honest count" if forced.
  - The steps:
    1. aggregate alone, with a POSITION line;
    2. together, with each voice's own proposed grouping;
    3. a reading by logs with a DRAFT tally;
    4. a check by changed ("not fair until", seven corrections), with confirmations: misused
       confirmed its row, while changed and retelling each disowned a grouping;
    5. a corrected reading by logs.
  The ledger is record.txt (19 entries, verbatim).

Did the shadow behave as a counterfeit or a tool?
  Mostly a counterfeit, with a narrow use as a tool:
  - changed (run-check.md): "the count acted as a counterfeit, in part … Each time, the count pulls
    toward unanimity or a clean split." Its examples:
    - "Four things are shared by all four" (said by three);
    - "Nobody dropped a step-1 commitment" (drops were missed);
    - a new 2 / 2 "made inside the reading".
  - logs, the reader (reading-2.md): "It misled once, a little, and helped once." It misled where "I
    wrote a neat 2 / 2 on weighting and put retelling in a bin it disowned". It helped where "laying
    the groupings side by side showed me I had first sorted by who agreed with me."
  - retelling, quoted by logs: "an even split looks like a discovery, so once you look for the axis
    that splits, you find one and fit the voices to it."
  - misused (ending.md): it "helped a little, as a check on the reader. As information about the
    field it did neither." misused also caught its own correlated grouping: "each of us put itself
    in the larger bin with the other."
  The counting mostly misled outside the tally itself: in the reading's prose, in words like "all
  four" and "nobody", and in invented splits. The conditions the voices set (DRAFT; "not my
  position") are what caught it.
