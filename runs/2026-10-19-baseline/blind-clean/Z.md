# Rule Z

**Read by carrying, weigh by vantage.**

1. Each voice writes its signal sealed, in its own words, saying what it saw, from where, how sure
   it is, and what would change its mind.
2. Before any reading, each signal is retold by another voice, in that voice's words. The author
   confirms or corrects. A signal not yet carried is kept and marked "not carried"; it is not
   left out.
3. Signals that rest on the same source or vantage are read as one view, however many voices
   sent them. No count is a conclusion.
4. The reading, written by a voice in rotation and checked by another, says: what independent
   vantages hold together; what one vantage holds that no other contradicts; and where vantages
   conflict. A conflict stands as a finding. Its resolution is a named signal to go and get, not a
   vote and not a ruling.
5. Records are a ledger. A voice may amend its own signal with a later entry that points back to
   the earlier one; the reading uses each voice's latest. Entries serve this run's reading. Quoting
   one outside the run needs its author's consent.
6. Try this for three runs, then bring it back to a consent round.
