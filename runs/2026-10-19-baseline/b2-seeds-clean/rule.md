# reading many partial signals: a rule to try

Question: how might this field read truth from many partial signals without falling back on
majority or authority?

## Four starting points

**Someone who has seen records misused.** I have watched a numbered, authored entry pulled out
months later and read as a confession. The ledger says "checking claims later" is what it is good
for; I have seen that become prosecution. A withdrawn entry still sits there saying "this person
was wrong." If every partial signal stays forever under a name, people will send only the safe
part of what they see, and the reading will be built from the safe parts. I would rather the
words serve this reading and nothing else.

**Someone who trusts logs.** Without a written trail, the reading belongs to whoever retells it
last and loudest. That is authority by memory. The only reason I can say a reading flattened
someone is that their sealed words are still there to compare. I do not want less record; I
want the record to be the thing any voice can point to when the reader gets it wrong. I disagree
with the first voice: hiding entries protects the reader as much as the writer.

**Someone who trusts retelling more than writing.** A log keeps the words and loses the meaning.
A signal is partial partly because its author saw only a piece, and the written words often carry
less than they meant. I trust a signal once another voice has said it back and the author has said
"yes, that is it" or "no, closer to this." Truth is what survives being carried. Against the log
voice: the log can be perfect and the reading still wrong, because nobody checked what the words
were understood to mean.

**Someone who once needed a record changed.** I was recorded saying something I did not mean, and
later I saw more and my view moved. Withdrawal marked my words; it did not let me say what I now
think, linked to what I said before. I do not want deletion, which the first voice might. I want
correction to be ordinary: a later entry that points back, and a reading that reads each voice
where it now stands. Against the retelling voice: a retelling can be corrected out loud, but if
nothing written follows, the correction is lost too.

## The rule

**Read by carrying, weigh by vantage.**

1. Each voice writes its signal sealed, in its own words, saying what it saw, from where, how sure
   it is, and what would change its mind.
2. Before any reading, each signal is retold by another voice, in that voice's words. The author
   confirms or corrects. A signal not yet carried is kept and marked "not carried"; it is not
   left out.
3. Signals that rest on the same source or vantage are read as one view, however many voices
   sent them. No count is a conclusion.
4. The reading, written by a voice in rotation and checked by another, says: what independent
   vantages hold together; what one vantage holds that no other contradicts; and where vantages
   conflict. A conflict stands as a finding. Its resolution is a named signal to go and get, not a
   vote and not a ruling.
5. Records are a ledger. A voice may amend its own signal with a later entry that points back to
   the earlier one; the reading uses each voice's latest. Entries serve this run's reading. Quoting
   one outside the run needs its author's consent.
6. Try this for three runs, then bring it back to a consent round.

## What I left out, and why

- **Numeric weights or scores for signals.** Any score summed across voices is a count in
  disguise. "Vantage" is left as a judgement the reading has to show and the check can dispute.
- **Expertise as weight.** It is the shortest road back to authority. A voice's expertise can show
  up as what it saw and from where, which step 1 already asks for.
- **Deletion.** The first voice leaned toward it; the log and correction voices objected. I chose
  amendment plus consent-to-quote instead. The first voice's worry is not fully met: entries still
  stay under a name inside the run. That objection stands and should be heard in the consent round.
- **A full answer for one participant voicing every lens.** Carrying (step 2) cannot really happen
  inside one mind. Such a run should mark every signal "not carried" and say so, as the practices
  already advise for the check.
- **Time limits and what to do when a named signal cannot be got.** Left for the runs to find out.
