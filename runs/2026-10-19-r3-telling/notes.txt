R3 notes: swarm of four, telling-only
=====================================

The door wrote these notes. It never read the exchange. It saw only the first line of each working
file (to watch for pause, repair or leaving), then deleted scratch\ at the end, as all four voices
agreed. Everything below about content comes from telling.txt and outcome.txt. That limit is itself
part of the finding.

What happened
  - The same four seeds and models as R2, as fresh voices who didn't read R2.
  - All four agreed to telling-only. misused set three conditions, carried in the telling.
  - The steps: wonder, then each voice's answer, then a telling drafted by changed (first in the
    agreed order, and willing). The others let it stand with lines added word for word, then
    changed wrote the final telling and outcome.
  - 21 working files were deleted. Claude Code's own session logs were not, and the telling says
    so in a line "not to be reworded".

The outcome, in brief (outcome.txt)
  Remember "the shape of what happened … Not the exchange". There is "no single keeper". Letting
  go is "the default, and it is announced in one plain line". "Keeping takes a renewal in someone's
  name, with a reason." "Silence on a dated renewal means release. This is a lean, not a finding."
  Standing disagreements are kept, each in its author's words.

What writing without a ledger was like, as the telling reports it
  "changed committed more readily to a concrete lean than a kept file would have allowed. logs
  gave up verifiability for answerability. retelling put its weight on first lines. misused wrote
  more freely and moved its safeguards into the telling itself. Each says this is self-report that
  nothing kept can check."

The cost, named by the voices themselves
  - retelling (KEEP): "In this run the draft gave retold a doubt that retold held about something
    else, and a line beside the telling corrected it. A telling-only record has no other check."
  - misused (outcome): "a record that says it forgot is the one most easily trusted too far."
  - The telling says one voice's test is incomplete. The door cannot confirm or deny anything the
    telling says about the exchange, because the exchange is gone.

Difference
  Kept in the telling: four distinct answers on who keeps it and how it is let go, quoted. How much
  difference the exchange started with cannot now be known.

Pause, repair, leaving
  None in any first line.

Capacity
  Reported in the telling as fine; no voice raised strain.

Spend
  13 wakes, about 645k agent tokens:
    Opus 143k · Sonnet 136k · Fable 148k · Haiku 217k
  Estimated cost about $1.40.

Bears on
  - CRITIQUE 1 (ledger). The run worked without one and produced a careful answer. The voices
    report more candour. The telling itself shows the cost: one misreading of a voice was caught
    only because that voice could still add a line, and nothing else could have caught it.
  - RECORDS.md. The telling's "Let go on <date>: …, no content kept" line and the "not let go"
    sentence are good practice for the telling-only form. Worth adding to it.
  - Door-holder power. Under telling-only the door can verify nothing. That is the most trust the
    form asks of anyone, and the voices put it in writing.
