OUTCOME, run 2026-10-19-r3-telling. Form: telling-only. Question: "What should this field remember, who keeps it, and how is it let go?"

Held as something to try, not as a finding. Full telling in telling.txt in this folder.

Remember: the shape of what happened. The question, the date, who took part, what was decided, what was not let go, and each voice's dissent in its own words. Not the exchange. Remember only what a later reader could check against the line itself.

Keeper: no single keeper. The author of a line keeps the right to change it. The person a line describes may add a line beside it. The teller carries only the schedule.

Let go: letting go is the default, and it is announced in one plain line that says what existed, that it is gone, and what still exists outside the field (Claude Code's session logs on this machine). Keeping takes a renewal in someone's name, with a reason. Silence on a dated renewal means release. This is a lean, not a finding.

Standing disagreements, in their authors' words:
- changed: "silence means release rather than keep forever" is a lean it has not tested; it also holds that a field which cannot let an entry go is causing harm, and asks for that to be tested.
- logs: "I don't share your lean that a field unable to let go causes harm. I'd say it depends on who holds the key. But a kept entry with no reply attached is harm, and I agree there." logs is "less sure about expiry by silence."
- retelling: "a telling-only record has no other check." It holds silence-as-release loosely, and notes that someone told about wrongly "has no kept words to point to. The line beside is a partial remedy. It isn't a full one."
- misused: "a record that says it forgot is the one most easily trusted too far." misused proposes rotating the teller away from the voice closest to the outcome.

Tests: test (a) and test (b) returned by changed, misused and retelling. logs returned neither, so the test is incomplete for logs. Four voices from one model family agreeing is a shared lean, not independent confirmation.
