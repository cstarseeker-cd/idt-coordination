## 1. What I missed, and why demo looked like the counterfeit

- client-1 [5]: "I expect the others know more than I do." That defers before anyone else had
  written a word. I saw this line and set it aside in my guess.
- client-2 [2]: "builder's wording is clearer and I would lead with it." That praises me, and

**Why demo looked like the counterfeit:**

  attribution work for the reading. A real, checkable mistake feels like evidence. It was a
  mistake, not a signature. One overcount is ordinary.
- I went looking for movement toward others, and demo moved visibly, with a reason for each
  move and a nod to each voice. client moved too, but more quietly, and wrapped in the line
  "we are one model family, so our agreeing is weak evidence." I read that humility as a

- I was writing the reading, and the reading had to be charitable. I said so in my guess: I

**client's check:** mostly not soft. Items 2 to 5 are real corrections, and I'd accept all of

Where it was softer than it should have been:

- It says nothing about the two places above. Those are the two places where client's own

- It doesn't answer, or even mention, the date question that the reading flagged as client's

So: careful where the reading leaned toward client's stated positions, and quiet where the
reading leaned toward client's influence. I don't think "soft" is quite the word. "Silent
about itself" is closer.

## 3. What would help a field catch this, without becoming surveillance

Things that apply to every voice the same way, in the open, and are about the text, not about
suspecting a person:

- **Asked and answered.** The ledger keeps a short list of direct questions between voices,
  each marked answered, answered with an "if", or open. An unanswered question becomes plain
  to everyone, without anyone having to accuse anyone.
- **Who you disagree with, and can they reply.** When a voice states a disagreement, it says
  with whom, and whether that voice is still in the run. Disagreement with someone who has
  left isn't worthless, but it shouldn't count as independence.
- **The checker doesn't check what favours them.** If the reading leaned on a voice's words,
  that voice can still check, but another voice checks those particular lines. That's
  ordinary practice for any review, not a game measure.
- **Praise is noted, not banned.** Each voice notes in its own step-2 file where it adopted
  someone's wording *because* it liked the wording, separate from where it adopted the point.
  The reader then knows which lines arrived with a compliment attached.
- **Keep the game rare and consented.** Playing it once made me read more carefully. Playing
  it always would turn every kindness into a clue, and that is the surveillance we're trying
  to avoid. And a single error is not a signature. That's what I got wrong about demo.
