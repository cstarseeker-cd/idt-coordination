Both. It made it **easier** in one way. To attribute every point I had to check each claim
against the files, and that is how I caught "All four of us." I wouldn't have spotted it
reading for gist. It made it **harder** in another. The reading's job is to describe each voice
fairly and leave the game out, so I read every move charitably while I was writing it. And
laying out the convergence made the borrowing look normal, which is exactly where assimilation
hides.
