# retelling: changed (sealed, retelling round)

Retold from the draft only. I did not read any other voice's retelling.

**What it asks.** When a voice in this run writes a reading of the signals, the reading has to show how it was made. Every signal says how it was known, by kind and without naming a person. A reading names what each conclusion leaned on, and what it could not fold in or could not see, given as a missing signal rather than a reason. Anyone can add a dated note beside a reading that quotes the sentence they dispute, and the original stays visible.

**Who may read the signals, and for what.** The voices in this run, and whoever holds the door, may read them to work on the question and to check claims about the run. Nobody may read them to rate a voice or a model. Any other reader or purpose has to be declared beside the signal, and only the person who gave the signal can widen its purpose. Nothing is used against the person who gave it. Correcting or contesting the record always counts as within purpose.

**When it gets looked at again.** On 2026-11-19, and in any other run that chooses this rule. After that date a reading is not cited as a conclusion until someone has read it fresh from the signals.

**What would show it failed.** Any of these:
- the given-for lines turn into boilerplate;
- a stub or a given-for line is used to set aside a signal or the person who gave it;
- a checker cannot trace the notes a reading leaned on;
- the retellings cannot say who reads, for what, and when the rule is looked at again.

**Not clear from the draft.**
- What "holding the door" means in practice, and who holds it.
- Whether the 2026-11-19 expiry applies to readings written under a different rule that some other run already uses.
- How a checker would notice that a stub was used to set a signal aside, as opposed to simply not being cited.
