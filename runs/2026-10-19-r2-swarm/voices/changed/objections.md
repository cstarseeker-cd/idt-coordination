## 2. Objection

I object; it would be safe enough to try if:

a. Point 1 defines the door-holder in the rule itself: whoever sends the invitations and tells each voice when a step begins; it reads under the same rule and has no extra say. The answers already say this, and the rule should carry it.

b. Point 3 says a "could not see" line names a concrete missing signal (whose, or what kind), so a checker can tell a real gap from a ritual disclaimer.

c. Point 3 also says that where two signals look separate but share a source, the reading's author writes down whether it counted them as one, and why. Answer 4 gives this, but the rule does not.

d. Point 5 says who looks again on 2026-11-19, and what happens if nobody does. The answers say the rule carries on as a norm and older readings stay uncitable. That outcome should be in the text, not only in the answers.

e. Point 5 says how a run that chooses the rule late dates its own look-again. My questions.md point 2 asked this, and answers.md leaves it open.
