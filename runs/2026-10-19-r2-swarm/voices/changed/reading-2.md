(voices/changed/aggregate-2.md, bullet "A retelling is a route"). ## 7. How much difference the run started with

But the shared starting material was large. All four read the practice files. All four read the other wonder and check-in files before writing aggregate-1, and each says so. Their aggregate-1 answers share a vocabulary (routes, given-for, the unfolded line, dated annotation) that was already in the wonder files. The shape-level agreement in aggregate-1 is therefore weak evidence, as the practice itself says: "agreement that began as agreement is weak evidence." The differences that survived step 2 (some of sections 2a-2e) are more informative, because they are where the four framings did not converge. Even those may be shaped by a shared family of models. I cannot tell from inside this run how much of the difference is independent.
