Questions to understand the draft, not objections yet.

1. Point 1 says "whoever holds the door" may read the signals. Who holds the door, and how would a reader know they are one? Is it a role in this run, or a person?

2. Point 5 says a reading is not cited as a conclusion after 2026-11-19. Does that apply to readings already written in this run, and to readings in another run that chose the rule after it was written? If a run chooses the rule late, does its own clock start then?

3. Point 3 asks a reading to name what it "could not see", given as a missing signal. Who decides that a signal is missing, the reader or the person who gave it?

4. Point 2 says routes that can't be told apart count once. What does a checker do when two signals look different but share a source, and the source is not named?
