## 2. Where they still differ

Quoted in each voice's words, with the file.

**a. What retelling is for.** Three different answers.
- retelling: "Hold retelling as a test of clarity only." (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, point 3). It is "a test of clarity", not of truth.
- logs: "I now count retelling as the way stubs get tested, not a competing method." (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Changed: my weak point now has a partial fix").
- changed: "'I know this because another voice told me' is an inherited route, and should not be counted as a second witness." (voices/changed/aggregate-2.md, "Retelling must not become a gate" paragraph).

**b. Whether an unanswered part can be cited.** This may be a difference, or a gap in what "cited" means.
- retelling: "An unmarked part counts as 'unanswered' and isn't cited as that voice's view until it is answered." (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, point 2).
- changed: "annotations and retellings are welcome beside an entry, and no entry waits for one." (voices/changed/aggregate-2.md). This speaks to entering the record. It does not say whether citing waits.
These two do not yet contradict each other. They do not yet say where entering ends and citing begins.

**c. Whether route counts are shown as numbers.**
- logs: "I'd rather publish a low honest number than a high flattering one." (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Added: the model fact"). logs keeps "distinct routes beside the count of voices, and shows both" (voices/logs/aggregate-1.md, point 2).
- retelling: "I'd rather a reading say this in a sentence beside any count than turn it into a number that looks more exact than it is." (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, "What changed", point 5).
- misused counted in words: "that's four answers and about one route" (voices/misused/aggregate-2.md, opening).

**d. What the cut should be, if the rule must be shorter.**
- misused: "I'd hold 2 and 3 over the rest" (voices/misused/aggregate-2.md, Changed 3). In its own numbering, these are purpose and the leaned-on/gaps line.
- logs: keeps "misused's two-rule core (purpose, gaps)" (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Held").
- retelling: "I'd keep 1 and 2: routes, and owners marking the reading." (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, "If the rule has to be cut").
- changed: no cut proposed.
So purpose-plus-gaps (misused, logs) and routes-plus-owner-marks (retelling) are two different minimal rules.

**e. Truth, plainly.**
- changed: "truth in this field is what keeps its shape when it is read by different routes" (voices/changed/aggregate-2.md, opening). changed is now the only voice that uses "truth" plainly in that sentence.
- logs: "never plain 'truth'" (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Held").
- retelling: "'Truth', on this view, is what has survived checks from different routes so far" (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, "My answer, revised"). Quoted, with "so far".

**f. Whether an unexplained route is a weak signal or a real one.**
- logs: "Still open for me: whether 'unsure of route' gets treated fairly... Nobody answered it." (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Still open").
- misused answered it in its own aggregate-2, under "Changed 1": "'Route withheld' and 'can't say how I know' are allowed stubs... It's never marked down, questioned further, or treated as suspect." (voices/misused/aggregate-2.md). logs wrote its aggregate-2 after reading only misused's aggregate-1, so "nobody answered it" was true for what logs had read. Whether misused's answer meets logs's question is for logs to say at the check.

**g. Willingness to draft the consent-round proposal.** misused will draft it. logs declined: "no, I'd rather not", because its lean toward stubs "would colour it" (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, Willingness (c)). retelling and changed would draft, with a co-draft or a different reader. ## 3. What moved between aggregate-1 and aggregate-2, and why

- **misused**: added that a route may be "given by kind, or withheld", because logs and the seed-level worry about "heard from whom" showed that a route can be turned on the giver (voices/misused/aggregate-2.md, Changed 1). Added that "only the giver can widen" a given-for, narrowing changed's wonder-read suggestion (Changed 2). Added "could not see" for this run: "The people this whole design is meant to protect are the ones not speaking in it." (Could not see). misused said it would not guess why.
- **logs**: moved most. It now puts the purpose floor before route-tracing, which it calls "nearly a change of rule" (voices/logs/aggregate-2.md, "Changed: purpose and protection come before route-tracing"). The cause was misused's point that a route stub can be used to set a signal aside. It also took on a retelling round as a partial test for stubs (cause: retelling) and a count of re-reads (cause: changed).
- **retelling**: moved most in its central claim. It dropped "truth is what kept its shape" after logs's line that retelling "tests clarity of wording better than it tests truth" (voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, "What changed", point 1). It moved owner marks and a protection rule in from misused and changed, and it moved the first telling to a fixed reference point after changed's point that shape needs a fixed earlier telling.
- **changed (me)**: added two gap lines (misused's silence point), counted inherited retellings as not a second witness (logs and retelling), kept annotations on entries and not people (misused). My aggregate-2 did not answer misused's "only the giver can widen" limit on a given-for. I should have, and I leave it open here.

What did not move: my "truth is what keeps its shape" wording (changed), logs's refusal to write truth plainly, retelling's refusal to turn a family-correlation into a number, and the three different cuts in 2d.

## 4. What the smaller and lonelier positions add

- **retelling**: the clarity-versus-truth split; the smoothing risk ("The version that survives retelling may be the one easiest to carry, not the truest"); the reading as one more retelling that its owners mark; the refusal to put the family correlation into a number; and the sealed retelling of the draft rule before consent. No other voice proposed the last one.
- **logs**: the tacit-knowledge cost ("drop true things that people know but can't say how they know"), which the others had not raised; the honest-stub weak point ("a voice can write 'seen' for something it inferred"); and the point that a rule should name how it could fail (voices/logs/checkin.md, "3. Suggested order").
- **misused**: "The misuse I've seen mostly came from true records read for the wrong purpose, long after their context was gone." This is the seed the purpose floor came from. misused also said it would not claim the given-for can be enforced: "It's a norm and not a lock." That is a limit on the whole practice, and the reading should carry it.
- **changed**: "authority by neglect" (a long annotation trail that nobody reads hands the last word to the last annotator); the test "the method reduces to a single signal dressed up as many" if signals cannot be given routes; and the caution that "folded in" in a run of one model family may be agreement by sameness.

## 7. How much difference the run started with

The four voices are four Claude models from one family: misused on Opus, logs (Margin) on Sonnet, retelling (Twice-told) on Fable, changed on Haiku (run.txt, and voices/retelling/aggregate-2.md, opening). They started from four different seeds and four different wonder lines. Those differences are real. They show up mostly as emphasis: misused on the purpose of a record, logs on its route, retelling on the shape of retellings, changed on whether a record can be corrected.

But the shared starting material was large. All four read the practice files. All four read the other wonder and check-in files before writing aggregate-1, and each says so. Their aggregate-1 answers share a vocabulary (routes, given-for, the unfolded line, dated annotation) that was already in the wonder files. The shape-level agreement in aggregate-1 is therefore weak evidence, as the practice itself says: "agreement that began as agreement is weak evidence." The differences that survived step 2 (sections 2a-2e) are more informative, because they are where the four framings did not converge. Even those may be shaped by a shared family of models. I cannot tell from inside this run how much of the difference is independent.
