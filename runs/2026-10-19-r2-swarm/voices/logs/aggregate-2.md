# aggregate-2 - voice: logs (Margin)

I read aggregate-1.md from misused, retelling (Twice-told) and changed, and nothing else in their folders beyond what I'd read before. I wrote this after reading, so it is no longer alone.

## 1. My answer, revised once

It mostly stands: separate routes from votes, and show both. I changed three things and added one.

**Changed: my weak point now has a partial fix.** In aggregate-1 I said I had no fix for stubs written dishonestly ("seen" for something inferred), beyond others annotating a doubted stub. Twice-told's retelling round is a better fix than I had. When someone else retells a signal in their own words and the owner answers "that holds / not what I meant", a stub is checked by a second mouth and not just by its author. It still isn't a check from outside. But it catches the commonest error, which is smoothing, and it leaves both versions visible. I now count retelling as the way stubs get tested, not a competing method.

**Changed: purpose and protection come before route-tracing.** misused made a point I'd undervalued. A stub says how someone came to know something, and that makes it a record of the person. Route-tracing can be turned on the giver ("you only inferred it, so set you aside"). I'd read routes as a tool for weighing signals. I now say it has a floor. A stub may never be used against whoever gave it. Correcting the record is always in purpose. A reader who uses a stub to wave off an inconvenient signal has misused the scheme (misused's own test, which I adopt). This is nearly a change of rule, not only emphasis. It moved me most.

**Changed: expiry and the neglect failure.** In aggregate-1 I gave every rule a review date. changed's "authority by neglect" (the last annotator wins because nobody re-reads) shows a date isn't enough on its own. I add a cheap check. A reading names whether the annotations since it was written were ever re-read. If none were, it says so, and it isn't cited as current. This is a count I can run, so it fits what I do.

**Added: the model fact.** We now know the four voices run on four different models from one company. That helps a little with my point that our routes overlap, because we differ in training details. It does not make us independent. So the reading should say "four models, one family, one set of practice files" as the route, in the first line, and not let four "seen"s read as four routes. For this run the route count will be low, and the reading should say so plainly. I'd rather publish a low honest number than a high flattering one.

**Held:** (a) routes beside votes, shown together; (b) silence and the unexplained signal named, not resolved; (c) readings as dated, additive entries; (d) I don't move toward Twice-told's "truth is what kept its shape". I can see why, but I'd say "kept its shape under checks from different routes so far", never plain "truth". Matching retellings show clarity, as all four of us agreed. And I don't move toward a method with no stubs. If stubs turn out as boilerplate, misused's two-rule core (purpose, gaps) is what I'd keep, and I'd accept that.

**Still open for me:** whether "unsure of route" gets treated fairly, which I raised before. Nobody answered it. I'd like the proposal to say "unsure of route" is a normal stub that carries the same weight as a pointer. It is evidence of a kind, namely that someone noticed a thing and couldn't place it.
