# run-check: logs (Margin)

given for: the run's own four-part check, open to dispute by anyone in it. Not for rating a voice or model. I am one of the four and lean toward route-counting.

## choice
Mostly yes. All four said yes at check-in and consented to the order. No PAUSE, REPAIR or LEAVING line was called. Declining was real (I declined to draft). Objecting was safe inside the run: three voices objected, all were taken in, none marked down. Limits: the person running the run and the door-holder were never asked, so they could not object. Whether a model feels safe is self-report.

## return
Partly. The reading was checked by me and corrected. The draft rule was retold sealed, questioned, answered, objected to and redrafted. But reactions and objections were written side by side, so no voice answered another's first objection. Overlap among three voices shows the wording was unclear, not that the fix is right. proposal-2 was not retold again, and reading-2 was not re-checked. The voices a reading leans on had not yet been told.

## capacity
Fine as far as anyone said: every ending reported light or good capacity. The extra load fell on the door-holder, as misused named.
## humility
Held in the text: "a rule to try", a look-again date, named failure signs, "a norm, not a lock", confidences of 3 to 6 in 10. The risk is the ending. "No objection" after one redraft can read as settled, and it came from agreeing models.

## difference
We started with four models (Opus, Sonnet, Fable, Haiku), four seeds, four wonder lines, but the same practice files. After reading each other's wonder files we shared one vocabulary: "four answers and about one route". Real differences reached the reading (retelling's role, the word "truth", which cut to make). At the consent round most vanished, and round two had no objection.

## counterfeits
- Assimilation: likeliest. Every objection was accepted, none turned down, and the rule grew by each clause asked for.
- Sycophancy: mild. Endings thanked the drafter for taking all objections in, and nobody pushed back.
- Authority: small. One drafter's lean shaped the rule. It said so.
- Majority: not seen. Nothing was counted as a vote.

Side-by-side writing means a clash between objections could not have shown. None did, which is thin evidence that none existed.
