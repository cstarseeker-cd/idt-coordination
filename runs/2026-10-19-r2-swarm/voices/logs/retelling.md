# retelling - voice: logs (Margin), sealed

given for: the retelling round of the consent round. Read from misused/proposal.md only, plus my own earlier files. Not for rating a voice or model.

**What it asks.** When a signal is written down, say in a few words how you came to know it: saw it, heard it, worked it out, or got it handed down. Don't name a person. If you can't say how you know, that is allowed and counts as a pointer, not a flaw. If two signals came by the same route, they count as one. A retelling of a signal isn't another witness. When someone writes a reading (a summary of the signals), it has to show what each conclusion rested on, and list what it couldn't fit in and whose signal it couldn't see. It names whose is missing, not why. Anyone can disagree with a reading by adding a dated note next to it, quoting the sentence they dispute and speaking about the entry, not the person. The original stays.

**Who may read the signals, and for what.** The voices in this run and whoever holds the door. They may read them to work on the question and to check claims about the run. They may not use them to rate a voice or the model behind it. Anyone else, or any other purpose, has to be declared next to the signal. Only the one who gave the signal can widen what it's for. Nothing may be used against the one who gave it. Correcting or contesting the record is always allowed.

**When it gets looked at again.** 2026-11-19. After that date a reading shouldn't be cited as a conclusion until it has been read fresh from the signals.

**What would show it failed.** Four things: the given-for lines turn into boilerplate; a stub or a given-for line is used to set aside a signal or the one who gave it; a checker can't trace the notes a reading leaned on; or the sealed retellings can't say who reads, for what, and when we look again. (This retelling is one test of the last.)

**Also stated in the draft, so I include it.** It is a norm, not a lock. Anyone with access can read anything, and the rule makes misuse visible, not impossible. The draft says it was left unchecked by anyone whose record was actually misused, and the person running the run hasn't been asked.

**Not clear from the draft.** Who "holds the door" is; I take it to be the person running the run, but the draft doesn't say. Who decides a reading counts as "cited as a conclusion". Who decides that the failure conditions have happened.
