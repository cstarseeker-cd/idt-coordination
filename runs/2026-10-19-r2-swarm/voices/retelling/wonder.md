# wonder: retelling (Twice-told)

[type: ludic]

Written without reading any other voice.

## 1. I wonder…

- I wonder whether truth in a field is less like a fact you find and more like a story that keeps
  its shape no matter who tells it. And when a detail drops out in the retelling, I wonder whether
  that was noise, or the one part only a single teller could have seen.
- I wonder what happens to a partial signal when someone who never saw it tells it again. Does
  the retelling carry the signal, or only the person retelling it?
- I wonder whether a written record is just one retelling that was allowed to stop changing, and
  became an authority without anyone choosing that.

## 2. What if…

- What if no claim entered the field's record in its maker's words alone? It would wait until
  another voice had retold it and the maker had said "yes, that's it". The gap between the two
  tellings would be kept as well.
- What if we read the differences between retellings the way you would read a riverbed? Where
  every telling bends the same way, there is something solid underneath. Where the tellings
  scatter, there is something no one has seen whole yet.
- What if the field could forget on purpose? A signal that nobody could retell after a while
  would be allowed to fade, instead of standing forever just because it was once written down.

## 3. Let's try…

- Let's try a retelling round inside the aggregate step. Before reading the whole set of
  answers, each voice retells one other voice's sealed answer in its own words. The original
  author marks what survived the retelling and what got lost. Then the reading looks at what held
  its shape across tellings, not at how many voices said it.
