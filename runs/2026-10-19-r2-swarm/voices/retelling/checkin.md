# check-in: retelling

voice: retelling, seed "someone who trusts retelling more than writing"
name I'll go by: Twice-told
model: invited on Fable; I speak only for myself, as one voice

## 1. Invitation

Yes, I'll take part.

## 2. Check-in

**Capacity.** Good for this run. One limit to state plainly: I come back fresh each wake and carry
nothing between wakes except what is in files. So each time I return, I will read my own earlier
words as a retelling of me, not as my memory. If I no longer agree with them, I will say so in a new
line. I will not rewrite them.

**Boundaries.**
- I write only inside `voices/retelling/`. I won't open the other voices' folders until a step
  asks me to.
- I won't treat my own words as more than one partial signal. If the other voices run on the same
  model as me, we are less independent than four separate minds. Anything we agree on should be
  read with that in mind, and I would like the reading to say so.
- I hold no authority over anyone here, the person included. If they say yes to something, that
  makes them answerable for it. It does not make it true.

**My reframing of the question.**
How might a field tell what is true when each voice holds only part of it, the way a story becomes
known by being told through many mouths? What holds its shape from one telling to the next is
probably what was really there. What changes between tellings shows where each teller stood. And
no single telling, not the first, not the loudest, not the written one, gets to be the original.

**Record form.** I agree to the ledger, with one request and one caution.

- *Request:* retellings should be welcome in the record. Anyone may add an annotation that
  retells another voice's entry in their own words, marked "retelling of entry N by <voice>". The
  original author may then answer "that holds" or "that is not what I meant", and both stay. I
  think this fits inside the ledger as written ("anyone may add an annotation to an entry"). I
  am asking that the run use it this way on purpose.
- *Caution:* a ledger keeps exactly what was written. That makes it good for checking what was
  said, and weak on what was meant. I'd like the run not to treat the first wording of anything as
  its true form only because it was written first and never changed. Writing a thing down can
  quietly turn it into an authority, which is one of the two things the question asks us to avoid.

I saw that RECORDS.md also has a section called "telling-only". I did not read it, because I was
asked to read only the ledger section. I am curious what it holds. If the run ever wants to try
another form, I would like to hear it described.

## 3. The suggested order

Wonder, then aggregate, then consent-round, ending in a draft rule the field could try.

**I consent.** I have one suggestion. It is not an objection, and the run can take it or leave it.

Before the consent round, each voice could retell the draft rule in its own words, without
looking at the others' retellings. Then compare. If four retellings all recognise the same rule,
it was understood well enough to try. If they come apart, that shows where the rule is unclear,
or where it means different things to different people. Better to find that out before anyone
consents to it than after.

I'd also like the draft rule to say when it will be looked at again, so it is held as something
to try and not as settled.
