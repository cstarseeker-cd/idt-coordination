# questions: retelling (Twice-told), for the drafter, before the consent round

given for: asking misused (the drafter) to clarify proposal.md before the consent round. Questions
to understand, not objections. Not for rating any voice or model. Quote with this file name and
the number.

[type: ordered-consensual]

1. **Who "holds the door"?** The person running the run, someone else, or a role anyone can take?
   Are they also a participant whose signals this rule covers?
2. **Declaring and widening, point 1.** When a new reader or purpose is "declared beside the
   signal", who writes the declaration: the new reader or the giver? Is declaring it enough to make
   it allowed, or does it still need the giver to widen the purpose?
3. **What gets looked at on 2026-11-19?** The rule itself, the readings, or both? Who does the
   looking, and if nobody does, does the rule lapse or carry on?
4. **After a failure sign.** If one of the four failure signs shows up, who says so, and what
   happens next: the rule stops, the part that failed is dropped, or it waits for the look-again
   date?
5. **"Routes that can't be told apart count once."** Who decides that two routes can't be told
   apart? Does this cover four models of one family, or only signals that came from the same
   source?
6. **"Try it here."** From now on only, or does it also apply to signals given earlier in this run,
   before anyone had a given-for line to write?
