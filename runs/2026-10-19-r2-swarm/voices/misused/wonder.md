# wonder: misused

Written without reading any other voice.

[type: ludic]

## 1. I wonder

- I wonder whether the misuse I've seen came less from false records than from true ones read
  for a purpose they were never given for.
- I wonder whether a reading of many signals becomes a new authority the moment it's written
  down: the signals fade, and the reading gets cited instead.
- I wonder which signals never arrive at all, because the people who'd give them have seen what
  happens to signals once they're kept.

## 2. What if

- What if every signal carried a short "given for" line, and reading it for anything else
  counted as a misreading, not as an extra use?
- What if silence were a signal too, so a reading had to name who didn't speak and what it
  can't see, with the same weight as what it can?
- What if a reading expired, so that after a while nobody could cite it and anyone who wanted
  its conclusion had to read it fresh from the signals?

## 3. Let's try

Let's try making each partial signal in a run carry a one-line "given for", and making the
aggregate reading show, beside each thing it concludes, which signals it leaned on, whether any
of them were read outside their "given for", and what the reading couldn't see.
