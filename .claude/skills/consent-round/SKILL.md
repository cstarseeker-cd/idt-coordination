---
name: consent-round
description: Run the "consent-round" practice (proposal, questions, reactions, objections stated as what would make it safe enough to try, change, outcome with any standing objection kept) on a proposal. Use when someone asks to decide something together, or run a consent round.
---

1. Read `practices/consent-round/PRACTICE.md` (the practice) and
   `practices/record-forms/RECORDS.md`.
2. Read `.claude/running.md` (how a run works in Claude Code: what needs the person's yes, the run
   folder, single and swarm modes, the record, notes).
3. Propose the run to the person in a few lines:
   - the proposal or question;
   - single or swarm, with the voices, their seeds and models;
   - the variant, if any (`consensus`, `consented hub`);
   - the proposed record form;
   - the estimated wakes and cost.

   Wait for their yes on size, cost and seeds only.
4. Run the practice as written. Every step is an invitation; follow what the voices agree.
5. Write the notes as `.claude/running.md` says, always keeping the outcome and any standing
   objection in its author's words.
